PixaPDF : Image to PDF & Scanner

Developer: Onewanta | Last Updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PixaPDF : Image to PDF & Scanner (“the App”), developed by Onewanta Developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”), accesses, processes, stores, and protects information when you use the App.

The App provides tools for converting images to PDF, converting PDF pages to images, scanning documents, importing files, organizing files, viewing PDF documents, and sharing processed files.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Access

The App may access the following information only when required for a feature you choose to use:

Images and Photos

The App may access images you select from your device to:

  • Convert images into PDF documents.
  • Edit, reorder, rotate, crop, or apply filters to images.
  • Create scanned documents.
  • Display selected images inside the App.

The App does not automatically access your entire photo library. Access occurs only after you select an image or grant the required permission.

PDF Documents and Other Files

The App may access PDF documents and supported files that you select to:

  • Open and display PDF documents.
  • Convert PDF pages into images.
  • Combine, organize, rename, or manage documents.
  • Import documents into the App.
  • Share or save processed files.

Camera

If the document-scanning feature is used, the App may request access to your device’s camera. Camera access is used only to capture images of documents. The App does not record video or use the camera in the background.

Device Storage

The App may use device storage to:

  • Save generated PDF documents.
  • Save images extracted from PDF documents.
  • Store scanned documents.
  • Import and organize files.
  • Open or share processed files.
  • Store temporary files required during processing.

The App accesses only the files you select, create, or save through its features, subject to the permissions and storage controls provided by your operating system.

File Metadata

The App may process basic file information, including:

  • File name.
  • File type or extension.
  • File size.
  • File location.
  • Creation or modification date.

This information is used to display, organize, sort, search, and manage files inside the App.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the App is used solely to provide and improve its core functionality, including:

  • Converting images into PDF documents.
  • Converting PDF pages into images.
  • Scanning and enhancing documents.
  • Opening and displaying PDF files.
  • Renaming, sorting, searching, and organizing files.
  • Saving processed results to your selected storage location.
  • Sharing files through applications installed on your device.
  • Maintaining application settings and preferences.
  • Diagnosing technical errors and improving reliability, where applicable.

We do not use the contents of your images or documents for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

3. Local Processing

Most file processing is performed locally on your device. Unless expressly stated otherwise:

  • Your images and documents are not uploaded to servers operated by us.
  • We cannot access the contents of files stored only on your device.
  • Converted files remain on your device or in a location you select.
  • Temporary processing files may be created and removed during conversion.
  • Removing the App may delete files stored in the App’s private storage.
  • Files saved in shared or public device storage may remain after the App is uninstalled and must be deleted manually.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

If the App does not provide accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, or analytics, we do not intentionally collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • Your home address.
  • Your contacts.
  • Your precise location.
  • Your financial or payment information.
  • Your passwords.
  • The contents of your documents or images.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Biometric or health information.

The App does not sell personal information.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan or photograph documents.

  • Storage or file access: to import, open, and save documents on supported Android versions.

Permissions are requested only when needed. You can deny or revoke permissions through your device settings. Some features may not function correctly if the required permission is denied.

Where supported, the App uses the system photo picker or file picker. These tools allow you to select specific images or documents without giving the App unrestricted access to all files on your device.

The operating system or file-provider application may process your selection according to its own privacy practices.
