Overview

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies regarding user data control, users of PixaPDF : Image to PDF & Scanner (developed by Onewanta) can request the full deletion of their account credentials and associated cloud data at any time.

We provide two convenient ways to delete your account: directly In-App via the application settings, or online using the Web Request Form on this page.

How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

1

Open the PixaPDF App

Launch PixaPDF : Image to PDF & Scanner on your Android device.

2

Navigate to My Account

Open the left drawer menu and tap on My Account (or Akun Saya).

3

Tap 'Delete Account' & Confirm

Scroll to the bottom of the My Account screen, tap Delete Account (Hapus Akun), and confirm. Your account credentials and Cloud Firestore profile will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Online Deletion Request (Web Form / Email)

If you no longer have the app installed on your device, you can submit an online request using the form below or send an email to pixapdf.one@gmail.com.
