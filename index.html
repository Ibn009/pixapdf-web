Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to commonly asked questions about PixaPDF.

Is PixaPDF 100% free to use?
Yes, PixaPDF can be downloaded and used completely free with no hidden subscription fees.
Are my document files safe and kept off the internet?
Absolutely safe! PixaPDF operates 100% offline in your phone's memory. Your documents are never uploaded or stored on any external server.
What file formats are supported by PixaPDF?
PixaPDF supports popular image formats like JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, as well as PDF documents for conversion and extraction.
How does the OCR text scanner feature work?
The OCR feature uses intelligence to detect text characters in photos or scanned documents, converting them into plain text that you can freely copy.
Does PixaPDF add watermarks to my PDF files?
Not at all. All generated PDF files belong purely to you with no added watermarks or logos from PixaPDF.
